# Usability test plan — not yet conducted

No students have been contacted and no participant data has been collected. This is a prepared plan, not evidence of adoption or improvement.

Before any study, obtain authorization to recruit and record, explain voluntary participation, and obtain consent. Use fictional coursework; do not request grades, diagnoses, school login details, real schedules, or identifiable assignment records. Give participants a clean fictional-demo workspace. Store any approved notes privately with an agreed retention/deletion date; never commit participant recordings or notes.

## Tasks

1. Add a fictional essay due in four days, with an outline and optional formatting step. Success: correct deadline and ordered steps, and the participant can find the saved assignment after reload.
2. Choose ten minutes and low energy. Find the suggested step and explain why it was selected. Success: participant identifies the deadline, time limit, energy fit, and first-unfinished-step rule.
3. Pretend a study session was missed. Preview shortening optional polish, then decide whether to save. Success: participant distinguishes preview from a committed change and sees that the deadline/required work stay fixed.
4. Start/pause a timer, reload the tab, and locate recorded study time. Success: participant understands that only elapsed whole minutes count and that the timer can resume in the same tab.
5. Export a backup and calendar deadline file; preview a fictional backup restoration. Success: participant understands what each file contains, replacement versus append, and where the pre-restore copy is found.
6. Open two tabs and save in one. Success: participant notices the conflict, exports the other tab if needed, and deliberately chooses the latest saved workspace.
